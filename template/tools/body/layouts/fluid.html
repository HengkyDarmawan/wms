About this layout

Every other page caps .nx-content at 1600px so lines of text never get uncomfortably long. On data-dense pages — wide tables, Gantt charts, board views — that cap wastes real estate on large monitors.

Use it deliberately. Body copy at 2,500px wide is genuinely hard to read, so reserve fluid width for tables and charts rather than prose.
