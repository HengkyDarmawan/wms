Analytics Intermediate 5.0 · 1,284 students

Most dashboards fail not because the charts are wrong but because nobody can tell whether the numbers are current. This course works through the whole chain: picking metrics that survive a reorg, wiring them to a source of truth, designing the page so staleness is visible, and getting a team to actually adopt it.

What you will be able to do

Choose metrics that stay meaningful for a year
Design a layout that scans in five seconds
Show data freshness without cluttering the page
Pick the right chart for each question
Instrument a legacy dashboard safely
Run a review that removes charts, not adds them
